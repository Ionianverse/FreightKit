Workflow concepts

Practical solutions for logistics and supply-chain work

FreightKit is building useful, lightweight tools around repetitive operations work. Start with a calculator or template, then define the workflow you want to improve.

Current status: these are solution areas and prototype tools, not a promise that a custom integration or managed service is already available. No account system, ERP connection, cloud database or paid checkout is active.

Freight cost & invoice checks

Compare supplier or carrier quotes, calculate chargeable weight, estimate landed cost and flag differences between expected and invoiced charges.

Compare quotes · Cost per kg · Freight audit template

Procurement & supplier tracking

Organise RFQs, purchase orders, supplier lead times, promised dates, open quantities and supplier performance in repeatable trackers.

Supplier scorecard · PO tracker template

Inventory & warehouse planning

Support reorder decisions, days of supply, stock classification, storage-cost estimates and operational exception reviews.

Safety stock planner · ABC analysis · Inventory template

Reporting & spreadsheet automation

Define repeatable input formats, reduce manual copy/paste, standardise reports and make discrepancies easier to review. Any automation should be scoped against sample files and actual business rules.

Additional service areas to scope

These can be developed as custom spreadsheet tools or workflow projects after reviewing sample inputs and business rules. They are not all live automated services today.

Document & data discrepancy checks

Compare invoices, purchase orders, packing lists, shipment documents or spreadsheets; highlight missing fields, mismatched quantities, units, dates and duplicate lines without assuming every file has the same columns.

Back-order & open-order reporting

Consolidate ERP exports, open order lines, inventory, incoming transfers and ETAs into an exception-focused report using rules defined for each business.

Shipment & delivery visibility

Track milestones, promised dates, late shipments, missing updates and action owners from customer-provided data or agreed system exports.

Warehouse & stock accuracy

Reconcile SKU/location files, highlight negative or inconsistent stock, monitor ageing inventory and prepare cycle-count lists.

Supplier performance & sourcing

Compare lead times, delivery reliability, price changes, quality issues and quote responses using transparent, editable scorecard rules.

Custom Excel, CSV & Power BI reporting

Build repeatable report templates, clean and combine exports, document formulas and prepare dashboard-ready data with clear validation checks.

Tell us what needs improving

For a useful request, describe the task, current file or system, inputs, expected output, exceptions and how often it runs. Do not upload confidential shipment, supplier or customer data to a public form.
