Safety stock and ROP.

Note: Assumes demand standard deviation is measured per day and lead time is in days, with stable lead time and an approximate normal-demand model. It does not include lead-time variability.

Formula

SS = z × σ × √lt. ROP = (avg × lt) + SS.
