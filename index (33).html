GTIN / Barcode Check Digit

GTIN check digit.

Note: Enter the data digits without the check digit. This calculates a mathematically valid check digit; it does not verify that a product code is issued or registered by GS1.

Formula

Alternate 3/1 weights; check = (10 − sum mod 10) mod 10.
