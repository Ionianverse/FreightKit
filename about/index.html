About FreightKit

Practical tools for everyday supply-chain work

FreightKit helps logistics, procurement, inventory, and warehouse teams turn recurring spreadsheet tasks into clear calculations and follow-up actions.

What you can do

  • Estimate freight, landed cost, container use, and inventory needs.
  • Compare supplier or carrier quotes and score supplier performance.
  • Review PO and shipment exports for overdue lines, missing ETAs, and exceptions.
  • Use CSV templates, Excel workbooks, and practical operating guides.

Designed around real workflows

Supply-chain work spans planning, sourcing, fulfillment, warehouse operations, and returns. FreightKit groups related tools so users can move from a question to a reviewable result: enter data, see the assumptions and calculations, then export a result or follow-up list.

Current tools process calculator inputs and uploaded CSV files in the browser. Saved configurations stay in the browser unless a user copies or shares a link.

What is available now

Free browser-based calculators, CSV utilities, 27 CSV templates, Excel workbooks, logistics guides, a custom-calculator builder, and file-based freight invoice and PO exception reports.

Scope: FreightKit does not currently provide live carrier rates, customs decisions, ERP connections, cloud workspaces, paid subscriptions, or automated supplier follow-up. Results support operational review and should be checked against current contracts, source records, and local rules.
