Container Number Validator

ISO 6346 validator.

Note: Checks the ISO 6346 number format and check digit only. It does not confirm the container exists, identify its current location or validate ownership/registration with a live registry.

Formula

Checks the ISO 6346 identifier format and checksum only. It does not verify that the container number exists or is assigned to a carrier.

FAQs

Owner code?

The first three letters identify the owner/operator code; the fourth category letter is usually U, J or Z.
