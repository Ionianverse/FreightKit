Freight invoice review

Match the accepted quote against the carrier invoice. Each finding explains what differs, where it came from, and what to check next.

Privacy: CSV, XLSX, and text-based PDF files are processed in this browser and are not uploaded to FreightKit. Scanned PDFs and images need OCR and are not supported in this version. Use one currency per comparison; differences are review signals, not contract decisions.

How to use the findings

  1. Review the totals, then start with high-impact invoice-above-quote and invoice-only lines.
  2. Use the source row or PDF page reference to check the original files. Expand What to check next for a suggested review step.
  3. Confirm against the rate agreement, shipment facts, and supporting documents before disputing. A difference is a review signal, not proof of an incorrect charge.

For PDFs, text must be selectable. Scanned or image-only PDFs need OCR and aren’t supported here.
