Freight invoice review

Match the accepted quote against the carrier invoice. Each finding explains what differs, where it came from, and what to check next.

Privacy: CSV files are processed in this browser and are not uploaded to FreightKit. Use files in one currency; this tool does not convert currencies or decide whether a charge is contractually valid. It compares structured CSV data; it does not read PDF scans or images.

How to use the findings

  1. Check the Review needed count, then start with invoice amounts above the quote and invoice-only charges.
  2. Open each row’s source line references in your original quote and invoice. The report shows why it was flagged and a practical next check.
  3. Confirm against the rate agreement, shipment facts, and supporting documents before disputing. A difference is a review signal, not proof of an incorrect charge.

PDF and scanned invoice extraction is not included; export or enter the line items as CSV first.
