Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. This notice describes the current static-first FreightKit site. Review and update it before enabling new hosting, email, analytics, advertising, or payment services.

Calculator inputs and CSV files

Calculators run in your browser. The invoice-audit and PO exception tools read selected CSV files locally in the browser; the site code does not upload those files to a FreightKit application server. You can download the resulting report to your device.

Browser storage and share links

Saved calculator inputs and custom-calculator settings may be stored in your browser's local storage. They are not synced to FreightKit or other devices. A share link can contain calculator inputs in its URL; anyone who receives the link may be able to see those values. Do not include confidential or personal shipment information in a share link.

Contact and suggestion forms

If the PHP form handler is configured on the hosting account, the name, email address, company (if entered), and message you submit are sent to the configured inbox by the host's mail service. FreightKit's current code does not save form submissions in an application database. The hosting and email providers may keep logs or messages under their own retention policies. Do not submit passwords or confidential customer, supplier, or shipment data.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and error logs under its own privacy terms.

Analytics, advertising, accounts, and payments

The current source does not implement analytics, advertising, user accounts, cloud-saved workspaces, online payments, or a paid subscription. If any are added, this notice and any required consent choices must be updated before they are enabled.

Retention and contact

Calculator saves remain in the browser profile until deleted or cleared. Contact messages are retained in the configured inbox under the account owner's and provider's policies. A monitored support channel and final business contact details must be confirmed by the site operator before launch.

Changes

This notice should be revised when the site, hosting, forms, or data practices change. The date above should reflect the latest material update.
