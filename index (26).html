Container Load Planner

Estimate cartons in container.

Note: Uses approximate internal container dimensions and compares two carton footprints. It does not account for weight limits, pallets, dunnage, door clearance or loading restrictions.

Formula

Estimated cartons = the better of two rectangular base orientations × complete layers. Uses approximate internal dimensions and upright cartons. It does not solve mixed-orientation packing and excludes payload, pallets, dunnage, door clearance and loading restrictions.

FAQs

Why lower in real?

Weight limits, pallets reduce capacity.
