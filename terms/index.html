Terms of use

Last updated: 25 October 2026. These terms describe the current FreightKit planning tools and should be reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction before commercial launch.

Planning tools and estimates

FreightKit provides calculators, templates, guides, and file-based review tools for operational decision support. Results depend on the inputs, units, assumptions, and user-defined rules shown by each tool. They are not a freight quotation, customs ruling, tariff classification, tax opinion, emissions assurance, engineering certification, or professional advice.

Verify decisions against source requirements

Carrier divisors and surcharges, freight classifications, duties and taxes, container limits, emissions factors, free-time rules, supplier commitments, and warehouse requirements can vary by carrier, country, product, contract, and date. Verify current rules with the relevant carrier, authority, contract, or qualified professional before making material decisions.

Files, formulas, and user inputs

CSV files selected in the browser-based review tools are processed on the device by the current site code. Users remain responsible for checking that source files are accurate and that mapped columns refer to comparable records. Custom formulas and scoring weights are user-defined and should be validated before operational use. Do not use the tools to process information you are not authorized to use.

Availability and limitations

The site is provided as available. Tools and downloads may change or contain defects. No live carrier-rate feed, ERP connection, cloud data storage, account system, or paid subscription is active in the current build.

Downloads and acceptable use

Downloaded templates and reports are provided for business planning and internal review. Do not misrepresent estimates as official determinations, interfere with the site, or use the service unlawfully.

Contact and updates

Form delivery depends on the hosting account's PHP and email configuration. The site operator should confirm a monitored support channel before launch. These terms should be updated when material features or service terms change.
