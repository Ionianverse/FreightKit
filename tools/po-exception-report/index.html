How priorities are assigned

  • Overdue: an open PO line has an ETA or promised date before the review date.
  • Missing ETA: the line remains open but has no confirmed arrival date.
  • Shipment exception: the matching shipment row contains a non-empty exception note.
  • Open quantity: ordered quantity less received quantity, floored at zero.

Use the report to assign a buyer or operations owner, record the next follow-up in your source tracker, and refresh after each update.
