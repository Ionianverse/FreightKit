Customs Duty & Import Tax Estimator

Estimate import duty and tax from entered customs value and rates. Actual treatment depends on destination rules and product classification.

Note: Illustrative estimate only. Tax base, customs valuation, duty rate and included fees vary by jurisdiction and product classification. Verify with an official source or customs professional.

Formula

Illustrative model: CIF = goods value + freight + insurance; duty = CIF × entered rate; VAT/tax = the selected base × entered tax rate; estimated total = CIF + duty + VAT/tax + clearance. This does not model country-specific exemptions, additional levies or every customs valuation rule.
