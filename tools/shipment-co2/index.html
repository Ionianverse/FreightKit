Shipment CO2 Emissions Estimator

Estimate shipment-related CO₂e from entered distance, transport mode, weight and emission factors; verify factors for formal reporting.

Note: Screening estimate only. Results depend on the selected factor, load utilization, vehicle, route, fuel and calculation boundary. Use a consistent methodology before reporting emissions externally.

Formula

CO2e = distance × weight × factor. Defaults: road 62, sea 8, air 500, rail 22.
