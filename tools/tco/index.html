Total Cost of Ownership

Compare total cost of ownership using purchase price, operating costs, maintenance and other editable cost assumptions.

Note: Excludes financing, downtime, inflation and discounting. Annual maintenance is multiplied by the life in years.

Formula

Undiscounted TCO = purchase price + (annual operating cost + annual maintenance) × useful life − resale value. Average annual TCO is total TCO divided by useful life. Financing, downtime, taxes, inflation and discounting are excluded.
