Freight Quote vs Invoice Audit

Compare freight quote and invoice line items using a shared shipment reference and charge code. Review higher charges, missing quote/invoice lines, and differences within your tolerance.

Privacy: CSV files are processed in this browser and are not uploaded to FreightKit. Use files in one currency; this tool does not convert currencies or decide whether a charge is contractually valid.

How to get a useful comparison

  1. Export line items from the accepted quote and invoice, or copy them into the sample CSV format.
  2. Map charge descriptions to comparable charge codes. Different labels can be mapped manually by changing the column selection; the tool does not infer that two differently named charges are equivalent.
  3. Review unmatched lines and tolerance exceptions against your rate agreement, supporting documents, taxes, and free-time rules.
