Demurrage & Detention

Estimate demurrage or detention charges from free time, chargeable days and entered daily rates.

Note: Flat daily rates are assumed. Enter detention days after any applicable free time.

Formula

Demurrage = (used − free) × rate × containers. Detention = chargeable detention days × rate × containers.

FAQs

Difference?

Demurrage inside port; detention outside.
