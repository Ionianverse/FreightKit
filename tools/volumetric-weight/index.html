Volumetric & Chargeable Weight

Calculate CBM, volumetric weight and chargeable weight.

Note: Divisor varies: 6000 for air cargo, 5000 for express couriers.

Formula

CBM = L × W × H × Qty ÷ 1,000,000. Volumetric weight = CBM × (1,000,000 ÷ divisor). Chargeable weight = max(actual, volumetric).

FAQs

Why different divisors?

Air cargo uses 6000; express uses 5000.

Which divisor?

Check with your carrier.
