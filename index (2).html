Parcel DIM Weight (Courier)

Courier DIM weight.

Note: Varies by courier.

Formula

DIM = (L×W×H) ÷ divisor. Chargeable = max(actual, DIM).

FAQs

Why DIM higher?

Space-based billing.

Divisor?

5000 express; 6000 some economy.
